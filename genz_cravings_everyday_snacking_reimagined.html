01 / WHO WE ARE

Not your average street-corner snack. We’re reimagining a classic.

Genz Cravings was born out of a simple frustration: why do the best local flavors always come in outdated packaging with mystery ingredients?

We took the iconic crunch of traditional plantains and gave it a modern, minimalist refresh. Rooted in farm-to-table simplicity and designed for fast-paced days, late-night study sessions, and everything in between—we don’t just make chips; we craft everyday culture.
